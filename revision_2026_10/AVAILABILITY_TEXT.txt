Repository availability statement
The saved prediction arrays, experimental settings, training and analysis code, 150 CKA records, derived tables and plot-regeneration commands supporting the revised manuscript are available in revision_2026_10 at https://github.com/kazuoishii/PPS-ID. Cite the publication commit or immutable tree link recorded in the publication receipt.

Original trained weights are not contained in the supplied supplement and are not uploaded. Recomputing CKA from hidden features requires the original 55 soft-only CIFAR-10 checkpoints and CIFAR-10 images; regenerating the plots requires only the included records. The checkpoint download location has not been supplied. Two three-update trial records per backend (CPU and MPS) and an audit script are public in diagnostics/. Their saved comparisons have been recomputed successfully. The original 100-update diagnostic logs remain unavailable; the shorter records do not verify its numerical maxima.
