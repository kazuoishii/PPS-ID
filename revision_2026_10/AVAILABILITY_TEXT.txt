Repository availability statement
The saved prediction arrays, experimental settings, training and analysis code, 150 CKA records, derived tables and plot-regeneration commands supporting the revised manuscript are available in revision_2026_10 at https://github.com/kazuoishii/PPS-ID. Cite the publication commit or immutable tree link recorded in the publication receipt.

All 44 BERT checkpoints are publicly available separately at https://github.com/kazuoishii/PPS-ID/releases/tag/bert-checkpoints-2026-10; they are not embedded in this supplement. CIFAR-10 checkpoints covering all 110 model records are now public in the repository directory checkpoints/cifar10/; see checkpoint_validation/CIFAR_CHECKPOINTS.md. Recomputing CKA from hidden features requires the original 55 soft-only CIFAR-10 checkpoints and CIFAR-10 images; regenerating the plots requires only the included records. The BERT release includes checkpoint configurations, paired predictions and SHA-256 metadata. Two three-update trial records per backend (CPU and MPS) and an audit script are public in diagnostics/. Their saved comparisons have been recomputed successfully. The recovered original 100-update CPU/MPS logs are now included and pass their own saved-record audit; the reported numerical maxima are verified.

Recovered 100-update records: diagnostics/100_update/; run `python code/audit_100_update_diagnostics.py`. This verifies saved comparisons, not a training rerun.
